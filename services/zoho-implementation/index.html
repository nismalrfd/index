FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to common questions about Zoho implementation.

What does a Zoho implementation partner do?

A partner analyzes your operational workflows, designs system architecture, configures applications, builds custom scripts, migrates legacy data, and trains your staff for maximum adoption.

How long does a Zoho implementation take?

Timelines vary depending on system complexity, module count, custom Deluge scripting, and migration volume. Projects typically range from several weeks to a few months.

Can Zoho be customized for proprietary workflows?

Yes. Through Zoho Creator, Deluge scripts, and custom API connections, we can build bespoke operational tools and widgets that precisely match proprietary business logic.

Can Zoho integrate with our third-party software?

Yes. Zoho connects to external ERPs, payment gateways, e-commerce stores, and database platforms via secure REST APIs, webhooks, and middleware layers.
