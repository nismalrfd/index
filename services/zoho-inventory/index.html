THE INVENTORY OPERATIONS GAP

When Stock Movement Becomes Difficult to Track

Businesses lose visibility when purchasing, receiving, warehouses, sales orders, fulfilment and accounting are handled across disconnected processes. The result is delayed fulfilment, inaccurate stock counts, and frustrated customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Zoho Inventory?

It is a cloud-based inventory management software that helps businesses handle purchases, sales orders, stock tracking, and warehouse operations.

Does RHenova implement Zoho Inventory?

Yes, we design the workflow, configure the system, migrate data, and train your staff to run their daily operations through it.

Can Zoho Inventory manage multiple warehouses?

Yes. It supports multi-location tracking and allows you to initiate transfer orders to move stock between different facilities.

Can Zoho Inventory support barcode workflows?

Yes, it supports barcode generation and scanning, which dramatically speeds up the receiving and picking processes.

Can Zoho Inventory track serial numbers and batches?

Yes. You can track individual high-value items via serial numbers, or track production lots and expiration dates using batch tracking.

Can Zoho Inventory integrate with Zoho Books?

Yes. They share the same underlying database for items, contacts, and financial transactions. When a shipment is made in Inventory, Books reflects the financial impact natively.

Can Zoho Inventory integrate with Zoho CRM?

Yes. Sales teams in CRM can view real-time stock levels and automatically generate sales orders that flow straight to the warehouse team.

Can you customize inventory workflows?

Yes, we build custom functions and automation rules to handle specific operational edge cases unique to your business.

Can you migrate existing inventory data?

Yes. We format and import your existing item lists, vendors, current stock quantities, and opening balances.

Do you implement Zoho Inventory for businesses in India and the GCC?

Yes, we support trading, distribution, and retail clients across these regions with their inventory deployments.
