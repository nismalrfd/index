Frequently Asked Questions

What does Zoho Books implementation include?

It includes chart of accounts setup, opening balances migration, template design, tax configuration, user roles, bank feed integration, and team training.

Can Zoho Books be customized for our business?

Yes. We heavily customize fields, transaction layouts, invoice templates, and use custom functions to handle specialized business logic.

Can you migrate our existing accounting data to Zoho Books?

Yes, we map and migrate your historical chart of accounts, customers, vendors, items, and opening balances from your legacy accounting software.

Can Zoho Books integrate with Zoho CRM?

Yes, Zoho Finance suite integrates seamlessly with Zoho CRM, allowing sales teams to generate quotes and view invoice statuses directly from the CRM.

Can Zoho Books integrate with Zoho Inventory?

Absolutely. Zoho Books and Zoho Inventory share the same database, providing real-time synchronization between stock movements and financial ledgers.

Can you automate invoices and payment reminders?

Yes, we can configure automated recurring invoices, auto-charge for saved payment methods, and establish escalating payment reminder sequences.

Can Zoho Books support GST workflows in India?

Yes, Zoho Books is fully equipped for Indian taxation. We configure the system to handle GST calculations, e-invoicing, and return filing formats.

Can you connect Zoho Books with third-party applications?

Yes, we leverage native integrations or custom API connections to link Books with your payment gateways, operational software, and external ERPs.

Do you provide support after Zoho Books implementation?

Yes, we offer ongoing support for end-of-month queries, new workflow creation, and continuous system optimization.

Can you implement Zoho Books for businesses in the UAE?

Yes, we configure Zoho Books to meet regional requirements, including corporate tax and VAT settings, for businesses operating across the GCC.
