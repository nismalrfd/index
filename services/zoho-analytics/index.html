Frequently Asked Questions

What is Zoho Analytics?

It is a powerful business intelligence and data analytics platform that allows you to visually analyze data from across your organization in real time.

Does RHenova implement Zoho Analytics?

Yes, we architect data pipelines, model relationships, and build custom management dashboards tailored to your business operations.

Can Zoho Analytics connect with Zoho CRM?

Yes, it natively connects to Zoho CRM, allowing you to build advanced sales pipeline and conversion reports beyond standard CRM capabilities.

Can Zoho Analytics connect with Zoho Books?

Yes, it syncs with Zoho Books to provide deep financial analytics, cash flow trends, and complex multi-departmental reporting.

Can you build custom dashboards?

Absolutely. We design specific dashboards for executives, sales managers, finance controllers, and operational teams based on their exact KPI requirements.

Can you integrate external business data?

Yes, we can connect third-party applications, cloud databases, and API feeds into Zoho Analytics to create a unified business overview.

Can existing reports and data be migrated?

Yes, we can import historical datasets from spreadsheets and legacy databases to ensure continuity in your year-over-year reporting.

Can dashboards be customized by department or role?

Yes, we configure role-based access so that different teams only see the data and dashboards relevant to their authorization level.

Do you provide ongoing support?

Yes, we offer continuous support for refining queries, adjusting data models, and adding new dashboards as your business scales.

Do you implement Zoho Analytics for businesses in India and the GCC?

Yes, we work closely with enterprises across India, the UAE, and the broader GCC to deploy scalable business intelligence solutions.
