Frequently Asked Questions

What does Zoho CRM implementation include?

It includes process mapping, system architecture, module configuration, data migration, user role setup, workflow automation, team training, and go-live support.

Can Zoho CRM be customized for our business?

Yes. We heavily customize fields, layouts, pipelines, and use Deluge scripting to build functionality specific to your unique operational needs.

Can you migrate our existing CRM data?

Yes, we routinely map and migrate historical leads, contacts, accounts, and deal histories from legacy CRMs and spreadsheets into Zoho.

Can Zoho CRM integrate with our existing applications?

Zoho CRM integrates natively with hundreds of apps. For specialized software, we use custom API integrations to ensure smooth data flow.

Can you automate our sales process?

Yes. Using Zoho Blueprints and workflow rules, we can automate follow-up emails, task creation, and stage transitions based on your criteria.

Can you integrate Zoho CRM with Zoho Books?

Yes, creating a seamless connection between your sales pipeline and financial accounting, allowing quotes and invoices to be generated directly from the CRM.

Can you build custom modules in Zoho CRM?

Yes, we create entirely custom modules to track specialized assets, properties, or project components that standard CRM modules don't cover.

Do you provide Zoho CRM support after implementation?

Yes, we provide ongoing support contracts for system updates, troubleshooting, and continuous customization as your business scales.

Can you implement Zoho CRM for businesses in the UAE?

Yes, we actively implement Zoho solutions for organizations operating across India and the GCC, ensuring configurations match regional requirements.
